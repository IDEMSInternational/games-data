Support

IDEMS Games puzzle apps, such as Rectangles

Contact us

If you have a question, a problem or an idea, please email IDEMS International at games@idems.international. If something went wrong, it helps to tell us which game, which phone, and what happened.

Common questions

How do I play?

Tap the ? button on the game's home page for a short tutorial. You can go through it again whenever you like.

Where are my games and records saved?

On your phone, in the app. There is no account, so if you delete the app or clear its data, your games, records and streaks are gone, and we can't bring them back for you.

How do I delete my records?

Tap 🏆 on the home page and then × beside a record. To delete them all at once, open the ☰ menu, then Game options, then "Reset my records".

Does the app need the internet?

No, every puzzle works offline. When it has a connection, the app checks for news about IDEMS Games, such as new games, at most once a day.
